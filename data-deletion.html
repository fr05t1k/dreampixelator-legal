Data deletion

Last updated: October 1, 2026

DreamPixelator stores only the access tokens of the accounts you connect, and only on your own computer. It keeps no copy of your data on any server.

How to delete your data

  1. In the app, open the System tab → Social accounts and press disconnect for each account. The stored tokens are deleted immediately.
  2. Revoke the app's access on the platform (see Privacy Policy → Revoking access). For Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business integrations → DreamPixelator → Remove.

Videos you already published stay on the platforms; delete them there if you want them removed.

To ask for help with deletion, email fr05t1k4@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.
