DreamPixelator

DreamPixelator is a personal desktop tool that publishes short vertical videos (Reels, Shorts, TikToks) made on the owner's PC to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Pages and Threads.

The owner picks a finished video, chooses the platforms and a time, and the app uploads it through each platform's official API. Nothing is posted without the owner's action, and the app does not read, follow, like or comment on anything.
