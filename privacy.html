Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

DreamPixelator ("the app") uploads short videos to your YouTube channel at your request. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data we access

When you sign in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos to your YouTube account. The app uses this access only to upload the videos you choose. It does not read, modify or delete your other YouTube content.

How we use and store data

Authorization tokens and the videos you select are used only to perform uploads. The app does not sell, share, or transfer your Google user data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
