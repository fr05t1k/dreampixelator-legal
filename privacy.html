Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

DreamPixelator ("the app") publishes short videos to accounts you connect: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Pages and Threads. The app runs on your own computer. This policy explains what data it accesses and how it is handled.

Data we access

When you connect an account, the app requests only the permissions it needs to publish videos:

  • YouTube (Google): upload videos to your channel, and read your channel name to show which channel is connected.
  • TikTok: your basic profile info (display name, open ID), and permission to upload or post videos to your account.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): the list of Facebook Pages you manage, the Instagram professional account linked to them, and permission to publish videos to them.
  • Threads (Meta): your basic profile info (user ID, username) and permission to publish posts.

The app does not read your followers, messages, comments, analytics or other content, and does not modify or delete anything already on your accounts.

How we use and store data

Access tokens and the videos you select are used only to perform the uploads you request. Tokens are stored only on your own computer and are sent only to the platform they belong to. The app has no server of its own that collects your data. The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising or profiling.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account in the app or revoke access on the platform. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens immediately. See Data deletion for how to remove all data.

Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business integrations
  • Threads: Threads → Settings → Account → Website permissions
